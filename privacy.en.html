Privacy policy

Effective from 12 November 2026

Family Cashbox («Семейная касса») is a free app for tracking personal and family spending and income. This page explains what data it stores, why, and how to delete it.

What data we store

  • Account: your email address, password (stored hashed — we can’t see it) and the name you entered.
  • What you enter: accounts, wallets, transactions (amounts, dates, categories, notes), goals, debts, payment templates, custom categories and the icon images you upload.
  • Settings: theme, language, currency, chart selection.
  • Technical data: the service providers the app runs on automatically keep service logs (IP address, request times) to protect against abuse.

We don’t collect location, contacts or photos from your device (except images you upload as icons yourself), and we don’t connect to your bank accounts.

Why

Only to make the app work: to show you your records, share a family account with your family and calculate statistics. The app has no ads, analytics or trackers. We don’t sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Who can see your data

  • Personal accounts are visible only to you.
  • Shared accounts are visible only to their members — people who joined the account with your invite code. They see the account’s records and your name.
  • The developer technically has access to the database and uses it only for support and bug fixing.

Where the data is stored

  • Supabase — the database and sign-in. Data is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS), and access is restricted by database rules.
  • Website hosting — serves the app’s pages and code. Your records are not stored there; like any website, the server sees your IP address when pages load.
  • App Store and Google Play — if you installed the app from a store, the store handles download data under its own rules. The stores do not receive your records.

Fonts and libraries are built into the app — no third-party font services or CDNs are used.

What is kept on your phone

  • A copy of your data for working offline, and changes that have not been sent yet. They are erased when you sign out.
  • Your background picture and this device’s settings.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprints (if you turn on the app lock) are checked by the phone’s own system. The app only receives “confirmed” or “not confirmed”; biometric data is never sent anywhere and is not available to us.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

Data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account and backup → Delete account. Details are on the “Delete account” page.

Deleting immediately erases your account, personal accounts with all records, debts, icons and settings. Records you added to shared accounts with other members stay with them — they are part of their family records; your name is no longer shown next to them. Copies in the database provider’s backup archives are deleted when their retention period ends.

Before deleting, you can download all your data: Settings → Account and backup → Download backup.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Children under 13 may use it only together with their parents.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about your data or the app: the project’s issues page on GitHub. Don’t post personal data there — describe your question and you’ll be contacted.

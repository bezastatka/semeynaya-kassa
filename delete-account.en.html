Delete your Family Cashbox account

You can delete your account and data at any time — it’s free and takes a minute.

How to delete

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account and backup.
  3. If you like, tap Download backup to keep your records.
  4. Tap Delete account, type the word DELETE and confirm.

Open account deletion

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen choose “With code” — a code will be sent to your email.

What is deleted immediately

  • your account: email, password, name;
  • personal accounts with all transactions, wallets, goals, debts, templates and categories;
  • uploaded icons and app settings;
  • shared accounts that have no members other than you.

What stays

  • Shared accounts with other members stay with them, along with the records you added. You are removed from the members and your name is no longer shown next to the records. If you created the account, it passes to the next member.
  • Copies in the database provider’s backup archives are deleted when their retention period ends.

Can’t access your account?

Open an issue on the project’s GitHub page — without personal data, just say you want to delete your account, and you’ll be contacted to confirm the account is yours.
